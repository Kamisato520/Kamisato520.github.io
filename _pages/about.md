<h1 class="main-heading">Qisong Zhang · 张启淞</h1>

<p style="text-align:center; font-size: 16px;" data-i18n-en="CV / AIGC Researcher · Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications &amp; China Telecom TeleAI" data-i18n-zh="CV / AIGC 研究员 · 北京邮电大学 &amp; 中国电信人工智能研究院 (TeleAI)">CV / AIGC Researcher · Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications &amp; China Telecom TeleAI</p>

<p class="lang-en">I am an incoming M.S. student at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), jointly trained with China Telecom's Institute of Artificial Intelligence (TeleAI) under the Excellent Engineer Program. My research focuses on On-Policy Distillation (OPD) for image and video generation models, controllable world generation, and goal-driven synthetic data engines.</p>

<p class="lang-zh">我是北京邮电大学与中国电信人工智能研究院 (TeleAI) 卓越工程师联合培养计划的待入学硕士研究生。研究方向聚焦于图像与视频生成模型的 On-Policy 蒸馏 (OPD)、可控视觉世界模型，以及目标驱动的合成数据闭环演化引擎。</p>
